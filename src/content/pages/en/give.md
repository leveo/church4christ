Everything we have is a gift from God. Giving is one way to respond with gratitude and to support the work of a church community.

## Giving with purpose

Generosity can take many forms: time, practical help, encouragement, and financial support. Contact the church to learn about its current ministries, priorities, and how gifts are used.

## A word to our guests

If you are visiting for the first time, please feel no obligation to give. You are welcome simply to be with us.

## How to give

Available online giving options appear below when the church has set them up. For other ways to give, or for questions about a donation, please use the church's published contact details.

Thank you for the care and generosity you share.
